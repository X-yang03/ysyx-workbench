## printf format string
- %d: print as decimal integer
- %f: print as floating point
- %s: print as string
- %c: print as character
- %p: print as pointer
- %x: print as hexadecimal
- %o: print as octal
- %u: print as unsigned decimal integer
- %e: print as scientific notation

## more format string
- %10d: print as decimal integer, at least 10 characters wide
- %10f: print as floating point, at least 10 characters wide
- %-10d: print as decimal integer, at least 10 characters wide, left-justified
- %-10f: print as floating point, at least 10 characters wide, left-justified
- %.4f: print as floating point, 4 characters after decimal pointer
- %10.4f: print as floating point, at least 10 characters wide and 4 characters after decimal pointer
- %08x: print as hexadecimal, at least 8 characters wide, with leading zeros
- %8s: print as string, at least 8 characters wide
